# Quiet Wayfinding

Form should carry a memory of moving through open space. A silhouette is more useful than a literal collection of outdoor symbols. The subject should be readable at a glance, with a second meaning revealed by the space it leaves behind.

Color should feel like daylight. Cool mineral green offers clarity; warm clay offers human warmth. Each composition uses a compact palette, carefully calibrated so the mark and its background belong together without sacrificing contrast.

Scale is deliberately generous, but never congested. Optical centering matters more than mathematical symmetry. Curves, margins, and the narrowest gaps receive painstaking attention, especially where a large illustration becomes a small everyday object.

Craft appears in the relationships between shapes. Unequal peaks, a narrowing passage, or a slight offset should feel natural and intentional. Meticulous refinement removes decoration until only a memorable contour and a clear rhythm remain. There are no captions inside the icon.
